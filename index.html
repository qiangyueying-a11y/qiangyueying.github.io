About Me

I am a Master's student in the Takemura Lab at the National Institute for Physiological Sciences (NIPS), Japan.

I received my B.A. in Psychology from Nagoya University, focusing on physiological psychology. My undergraduate research involved measuring physiological signals—such as respiration and heart rate patterns—alongside behavioral tasks.

Broadly, my current research interests lie in human visual perception, white matter pathways, and structure-function connectivity in the human brain.

In my free time, I enjoy wandering through art museums, exploring teas from around the world, and soaking up the afternoon sunshine. As a Chinese saying goes, "Stealing time out of a busy life makes the leisure all the sweeter."
